How to Stay Safe

Recommended defensive security practices

🔑

Use MFA

Enable multi-factor authentication on email, exchanges, cloud accounts and other important services.

🎣

Watch for phishing

Never enter a wallet seed phrase or password after following an unexpected link or message.

👛

Protect your wallet

Never put private keys or seed phrases into a website, JavaScript variable, database or analytics system.

🔄

Keep software updated

Regularly update browsers, wallets, operating systems and security dependencies.

🧾

Verify transactions

Carefully verify recipient addresses, contract interactions and transaction amounts before signing.

🚫

Don't trust urgency

Attackers often create fake emergency messages asking you to immediately move funds or reveal credentials.
